Data notes

  • Blank = no data. Nothing is filled with zeros or guesses. Negative P/Es are blank and excluded from means and medians.
  • ≈ italic = approximation. True point-in-time analyst consensus history isn't free, so historical forward P/E is price ÷ the adjusted EPS the company actually reported over the following four quarters (Alpha Vantage), with the not-yet-reported last ~12 months filled from today's consensus. Treat these as estimates.
  • Trailing P/E = price ÷ GAAP diluted EPS, last four quarters (SEC filings, point-in-time). Foreign filers (20-F) and non-US listings use Yahoo's trailing EPS for the current value; their history is often blank.
  • ‡ forward history for this stock is built from GAAP EPS because its adjusted-EPS backfill (Alpha Vantage free tier, 25 calls/day) hasn't reached it yet. GAAP EPS is usually lower than adjusted, so these medians run high and the comparison looks too cheap. It fixes itself within a few days.
  • † price is the last close, not 9:45 ET (market closed, e.g. Tokyo or Seoul).
  • Debt safety (SEC filings first, Yahoo fallback; banks, insurers and REITs are skipped): net debt / EBITDA = (debt − cash & short-term investments) ÷ trailing-12-month (operating income + D&A). Negative = net cash. Green below 2, red above 4. Interest cover = operating income ÷ interest expense (red below 2, green 5+). Y = from Yahoo (used when SEC data is missing, e.g. non-US filers; Yahoo gives no interest cover). Blank = no data or negative EBITDA. Captive-finance arms (e.g. GM, Ford) can look too safe because their debt is tagged differently.
  • n/m = not meaningful: a P/E above 200 (earnings are tiny, so the ratio says nothing). It is treated as blank in colours, filters and the "vs median" columns; hover the cell to see the raw number.
  • 5y/10y statistics are shown only when at least 80% of trading days in the window have a valid P/E.
  • Fwd P/E is price ÷ Yahoo consensus EPS for the next fiscal year. Every day's real value is saved, so real history replaces the approximation over time.
  • Colours on the current Trailing / Forward P/E: green = lower than all four of its own history figures (5y & 10y mean and median), red = higher than at least one of them. Blank history figures are ignored; no colour only if all four are blank. The "vs median" columns are green when cheaper than that median, red when more expensive.
